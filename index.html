Put THIRTY on your Home Screen

iPhone has no install button for web apps, so it takes three taps in Safari:

  1. Tap the Share button ⎙ at the bottom of Safari.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. Open THIRTY from the icon, not from Safari.

Opened from the icon it runs full screen, keeps its own storage, and the last thirty cards stay readable offline.
